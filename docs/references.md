# Scientific References — MAAA

## Metacognitive Autopoietic Adaptive Agent

---

## 1. Autopoiesis and Embodied Cognition

**Maturana, H., & Varela, F. (1980)**  
*Autopoiesis and Cognition*  
→ Operational closure, structural coupling, self-maintenance

**Varela, F., Thompson, E., & Rosch, E. (1991)**  
*The Embodied Mind*  
→ Cognition as embodied action; shared embodiment foundation

**Brooks, R. A. (1991)**  
*Intelligence without representation. Artificial Intelligence, 47(1–3)*  
→ Behaviour-based robotics; reactive agents without central world model

---

## 2. Extended Mind and Human-AI Teaming

**Clark, A., & Chalmers, D. (1998)**  
*The Extended Mind. Analysis, 58(1), 7–19*  
→ MAAA as external metacognitive organ; cognitive boundary extension

**Klein, G. et al. (2004)**  
*Ten challenges for making automation a "team player" in joint human-agent activity*  
*IEEE Intelligent Systems, 19(6)*  
→ Human-AI teaming in high-stakes environments

**Woods, D. D. (2015)**  
*Four concepts for resilience and the implications for the future of resilience engineering*  
*Reliability Engineering & System Safety, 141*  
→ Adaptive capacity under stress; graceful degradation

---

## 3. Cognitive Load and Emergency Decision-Making

**Sweller, J. (1988)**  
*Cognitive load during problem solving. Cognitive Science, 12(2)*  
→ Cognitive Load Theory; basis of MAAA entropy reduction design

**Klein, G. A. (1998)**  
*Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions*  
→ Recognition-primed decision-making under time pressure

**Kahneman, D. (2011)**  
*Thinking, Fast and Slow*  
→ System 1/2 dynamics under stress; MAAA supports System 2 when System 1 dominates

---

## 4. AR and Wearable Cognition

**Billinghurst, M. et al. (2015)**  
*A survey of augmented reality. Foundations and Trends in HCI, 8(2–3)*  
→ AR as cognitive extension in operational environments

**Feiner, S. K. (2002)**  
*Augmented reality: A new way of seeing. Scientific American, 286(4)*  
→ AR overlay for situational awareness

---

## 5. Stress Detection and Human State Monitoring

**Giannakakis, G. et al. (2022)**  
*Review on psychological stress detection using biosignals*  
*IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing, 13(1)*  
→ Voice, facial expression, HRV as stress indicators

**Ekman, P., & Friesen, W. V. (1978)**  
*Facial Action Coding System (FACS)*  
→ Foundation of micro-expression analysis in L3
